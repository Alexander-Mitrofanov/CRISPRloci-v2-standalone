# Council 15 — Phigaro-v2 / prophage_context

Date: 2026-10-14. Scope: Phigaro-v2 2.4.1 at `b4f32844b83167afa3ee1b1d6dea994d487bd7ec`, its integration, independent validation, publication and handoffs. The chair reviewed platform boundaries; two independent specialists reviewed author/science intent and evidence/output connections, then exchanged challenges and rebuttals. Both completed their review. No implementation, reference acquisition, runtime update or production operation occurred. Detector reports were inspected as input contracts; the excluded detector and CasAndra were not audited.

## Four verdicts

| Axis | Verdict |

| Author-intended integration | **Substantially faithful within a narrower declared workflow; input-scope disclosure needs repair.** Preserve the basic pVOG/GC method, native GC covariate and source-derived exact-match interpretation. ## Scientific method and assessed scope

The [Phigaro paper](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/36/12/3882/5822875) and [author repository](https://github.com/bobeobibo/phigaro) describe gene prediction, viral-profile annotation and GC-aware triangular smoothing. The paper's basic settings are window 32, thresholds 45.39/46.0, E-value 0.00445 and black/white adjustments −2.2/+0.7. These settings survive in `tools/Phigaro-v2/phigaro/bounded.py:23` and the original smoothing implementation at `phigaro/finder/v2.py:40`. The wrapper scores each source separately and retains inclusive terminal genes (`bounded.py:243`). This is candidate-region evidence; neither induction nor active CRISPR targeting follows from it (`bounded.py:40`). Published performance estimates are not newly measured accuracy for this bounded distribution.

Prodigal's reported `gc` and exact source-derived `source_gc` are intentionally different fields (`bounded.py:165`). The former enters the inherited scoring calculation; the latter independently describes the CDS. A fresh Prodigal control on lambda yielded 61 genes, with 12 native GC values differing from exact source GC by more than 0.001; the maximum difference was 0.011111. Substituting `source_gc` would change the model covariate and potentially its thresholds. Preserve both; evaluating such a substitution belongs to a separate scientific study.

One limitation warrants prominent submission guidance. All eligible records enter one Prodigal `-p single -g 11` invocation (`bounded.py:323`), although region smoothing is per source. The [Prodigal author's input advice](https://github.com/hyattpd/Prodigal/wiki/Advice-By-Input-Type) cautions against normal-mode FASTA containing unrelated genomes and discusses uncertainty for short training inputs. Current submission wording merely says “submitted sequences” (`frontend/src/science.js:24`), with 20-record/2-Mb limits (`frontend/src/features/submission/useAnalysisForm.js:59`). A fresh controlled probe added the first 100 kb of existing NC_017040.1 to unchanged lambda: lambda calls changed from 61 to 64, with 29 coordinate/strand/protein tuples in the symmetric difference. This establishes joint-training dependence, **not** degraded accuracy or altered prophage calls. Explain that records should form one genome/assembly suitable for joint training, or closely related strains; reserve alternative training modes for evaluation.

Records below 20,000 nt are explicitly `ineligible_short_sequence` (`bounded.py:323`, `:389`). Both validators reconstruct that scope, and the UI distinguishes zero assessed records from assessed negative results (`backend/src/crispr_web/execution/validation_prophage_evidence.py:653`; `frontend/src/components/results/annotations/ProphageContextResults.vue:48`). Do not lower the cutoff automatically or describe ≥20 kb as a reliability guarantee. Short-source spacer matching remains a separate analysis.

## References and native execution

Provisioning specifies Prodigal 2.6.3/HMMER 3.4 and locked Python dependencies (`scripts/provision_tools.py:42`, `:98`). The worker receives explicit binary/model paths; bounded prediction does not read the legacy YAML configuration. No analogous ambient-configuration defect was demonstrated here.

The full pVOG concatenation has **9,518 profiles, 767,855,419 bytes**, SHA-256 `0ddeafff975f1cfd86686e95f52c7a9980f9ad10fa082797313097ca42be97ed`. Operator provisioning pins the [NCBI archive](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pub/kristensen/pVOGs/downloads/All/AllvogHMMprofiles.tar.gz), checks archive/member inventory and concatenation identity, and verifies executable/reference receipts (`scripts/runtime/context_tools.py:18`, `:145`, `:157`, `:200`). Job execution does not download models. Independent validators require the exact reference and policy (`Integration/crispr_integration/prophage_evidence.py:645`; backend counterpart `:678`). No missing subset of required profiles was demonstrated in this design. A fresh full-model search was unavailable because the prior local full pVOG asset was not present; stored lambda evidence is not proof that a current deployment is provisioned.

[PHROGs](https://phrogs.lmge.uca.fr/) supplies broader remote-homology families and standardized functional annotations; its [primary publication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8341000/) describes a different clustering construction. Different identifiers, family definitions and annotation coverage cannot inherit pVOG-specific penalty lists, score distributions or cutoffs. Before any replacement or enlargement, pin exact bytes and licenses, map profile semantics, choose compatible search settings, recalibrate on a development panel, and evaluate held-out host/taxon, GC and contig-length strata plus negatives. Report interval precision/recall, boundary accuracy, resource cost and failure rates. No benefit was measured here, and no model change is approved by this council.

## Evidence, validation and public output

Integration passes canonical source FASTA to Phigaro, then runs source-spacer search with same-record scope, zero substitutions and bounded work (`Integration/crispr_integration/prophage_context.py:26`, `:50`). Source-bound query reconstruction checks hashes, coordinates, architecture, deleted slots and eligibility (`source_spacer_evidence.py:27`). Eligible queries are unambiguous 16–1000-nt occurrences in Bona-fide/Possible arrays. Replay checks every forward/reverse exact full-length match, preserves palindromic `both`, and excludes overlaps with **all reported arrays**, including other arrays (`:165`, `:195`). Region links mean any inclusive interval overlap, not containment (`:282`). Matching does not evaluate PAMs or assign biological targeting confidence.

The backend independently reconstructs native genes, translations, best hits, scoring and regions, then independently reconstructs the search/context from original inputs (`backend/src/crispr_web/execution/validation_prophage_context.py:19`; `validation_prophage_evidence.py:478`, `:592`). It does not import Integration validators. These gates detect source, interval, policy and evidence tampering without rerunning HMMER during routine validation.

Full `prophage-context.json` contains genes, candidate sequences, source arrays/spacers and candidate matches; `result.json` projects that full export (`backend/src/crispr_web/publication/prophage_context.py:12`; `publication/compact/prophage_context.py:4`). The preview separately caps records, prioritizes crosswalks for displayed matches and flags truncation (`summaries/prophage_context.py:48`). A fresh zero-row preview probe retained one region and 61 genes in compact output. This payload has scientific value; its size alone is not a defect under `docs/module-results.md:86`.

Two small shortcomings remain. Spreadsheet protection currently transforms controlled TSV strands `+`/`-` into `'+`/`'-` (`publication/prophage_context.py:56`), reproduced with the real writer. Also, `context_document` exports inventories/counts without its enforced search policy (`Integration/crispr_integration/source_spacer_evidence.py:302`). Compact Phigaro parameters do not explain query eligibility, exactness, array exclusion, exhaustive completed scope or unevaluated PAMs. These are interpretive semantics, not redundant runtime metadata.

Raw `phigaro/` and `source-spacer/` directories are excluded from ZIP discovery (`publication/contracts.py:62`; `discovery.py:70`). Do not promise downloadable raw excluded-match rows. The council rejected treating their omission as corruption: inventories, aggregate exclusion counts, original input and an explicit deterministic policy support the compact purpose. Extra sanitized audit tables are optional.

## Handoffs, deliberation and accepted actions

`prophage_context` accepts target ports, but `write_sequence_exports` excludes it from derived exports (`backend/src/crispr_web/publication/sequence_ports.py:115`). A fresh writer probe emitted only `input-sequences.json`, which replays original input. Neither region sequences nor `result.json` are current submission ports. This conflicts with the broad detection-containing claim in `docs/module-results.md:123`.

The output specialist initially favored correcting documentation only. The chair challenged this with a direct reuse experiment: independently validate the saved prophage-spacer fixture, then feed its canonical reports to existing `array_documents(mode="prophage_context")`. It produced one array, two repeats and one spacer with complete/source-forward native origins. Existing importers accepted all five repeat/spacer destinations and the array-comparison document. The specialist accepted promotion to an engineering repair. No detector execution, homology assertion or new grouping was needed.

Required follow-through, in priority order:

1. **Preserve TSV enums.** Exempt only validated strand values `+`, `-`, `both`; test exact TSV/JSON agreement and continued escaping of hostile identifiers.
2. **Enable established derived ports.** Reuse the current source-verifying builder and update output declarations/documentation. Preserve accepted category filtering, native origins, missing orientation, deletions, completeness and caps. Distinguish exported accepted occurrences from the stricter fixed match-query eligibility. Test tampering, incomplete ports, destination limits and browser continuation to existing consumers; never fabricate array origins for candidate regions.
3. **Publish bounded search semantics.** Add effective scope, eligibility, exactness, all-array exclusion, completion and PAM/targeting limitations to sanitized full/compact evidence, propagating the contract independently through both validators and fixtures. Test no-hit, excluded-query, short-source and palindromic cases.
4. **Clarify training scope before submission and in methods.** State joint single-mode training, code 11 and short-record eligibility without promising calibrated performance. Test that the explanation is visible; do not change calls or thresholds.

The science specialist challenged arbitrary multi-genome suitability; all members accepted documentation now and evaluation before a gene-calling change. All rejected deleting gene evidence, substituting source GC, enlarging models automatically or inferring activity from overlap.
