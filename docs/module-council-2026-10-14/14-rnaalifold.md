## Intended science and required resources

The wrapper explicitly limits interpretation to a single homologous family, not a repeat–anti-repeat duplex, transcription prediction or activity assay (`tools/RNAalifold-v2/README.md:3–18`). It accepts 2–50 unambiguous sequences of 30–500 bases, requires at least two distinct RNA sequences, and caps the alignment at 1,000 columns. DNA is converted T→U without reverse complementation. Exact RNA duplicates receive one representative weight while every original identity and membership is retained (`rna_family_tool/core.py:10–17,32–81`). Related nonidentical sequences remain equally weighted, so close relatives can still dominate the sample.

The actual command fixes MAFFT FFT-NS-2 with nucleotide mode, input order, two guide-tree calculations and no iterative refinement (`rna_family_tool/cli.py:19–35`). The comparative compound explicitly selects Turner2004, 37°C, covariance/noncanonical factors of one, and the other model defaults; it snapshots native thermodynamic/Boltzmann tables before MFE and partition-function evaluation (`core.py:139–190`). Fresh-process oracle tests deliberately disturb process-global ViennaRNA defaults (`tests/test_boundaries.py:20–74`). This is relevant evidence of controlled settings, unlike assuming another tool’s configuration defect applies here.

The [RNAalifold tutorial](https://www.tbi.univie.ac.at/RNA/ViennaRNA/doc/html/tutorial/RNAalifold.html) explains why a comparative score combines sequence energies and covariance, and why pair probabilities expose structural alternatives. Accordingly, this score is not the free energy of one supplied RNA. Probabilities are model ensemble quantities, not experimental probabilities of activity. The report retains every MFE pair and other pairs with probability ≥0.01; omitted alternatives are below the reporting threshold, not necessarily zero (`core.py:177–187`). Compensatory counts enumerate pairs of observed representatives with canonical pairs differing at both positions; they are neither inferred evolutionary events nor significance tests (`core.py:112–121`).

Provisioning pins MAFFT 7.526 and ViennaRNA 2.7.0 and checks their native receipts (`scripts/provision_tools.py:55,114–119,138–160`; `scripts/runtime/context_tools.py:215,244–249`). No BLAST database, homology model or genome collection is required by this bounded calculation. Published comparative tracrRNA work first curated homologues before alignment/folding and separately assessed transcription; that broader research process does not make all its tools dependencies of this wrapper ([Gasiunas et al., 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-19344-1)). Beneficial family expansion requires compatible RNA identity, verified orientation, comparable boundaries and useful diversity. More near-duplicates can worsen sampling imbalance. The [upstream manual](https://www.tbi.univie.ac.at/RNA/ViennaRNA/doc/html/man/RNAalifold.html) warns about redundancy and uneven relatedness; its currently served 2.7.2 page supports interpretation, while local 2.7.0 execution supplies version-specific evidence.

## Independent gates and demonstrated repairs

Integration independently reconstructs source hashes, duplicate groups, alignment columns, consensus, structure, probability bounds/mass and descriptive pair counts before accepting the stage (`Integration/crispr_integration/rna_family.py:50–140,143–169`). Backend repeats these checks without importing Integration or the scientific engine, additionally binding the report to immutable input and native alignment/fold files (`backend/src/crispr_web/execution/validation_rna_family.py:49–187`). These checks establish consistency and source binding; they are not a second numerical folding implementation or proof of biological correctness.

**P1 — prevent ambient MAFFT helper substitution.** Both MAFFT subprocesses inherit the environment (`rna_family_tool/cli.py:23–29`). The worker copies its environment and installs job-specific temporary paths; the generated runtime wrapper clears `PYTHONPATH` but not `MAFFT_BINARIES` (`backend/src/crispr_web/worker.py:479–491`; `scripts/provision_tools.py:124–134`). The [MAFFT manual](https://mafft.cbrc.jp/alignment/software/manual/manual.html) documents this helper-directory override. A harmless fresh probe supplied a temporary helper directory containing symlinks to the pinned helpers and a transparent `disttbfast` wrapper that recorded invocation before executing the original helper. With the absolute pinned launcher, the marker executed outside its installation while the report still identified 7.526 and produced the normal 4-source/3-representative result. This proves execution-identity drift, not incorrect biology or an anonymous-request exploit. Valid source-bound output cannot attest which helper ran.

Acceptance: create a copied child environment without inherited `MAFFT_BINARIES` for **both** version and alignment commands; leave the caller environment, explicit executable and worker temporary settings intact. The marker must remain uncalled; actual managed/relocated 7.526 launchers must resolve their intended helpers; paired/unpaired results and both gates must remain unchanged. An installation requiring a helper override needs explicit, inventoried operator configuration. No blanket environment reset or unproven `MAFFT_TMPDIR` containment claim is warranted.

The same generated wrapper serves other managed MAFFT consumers (`scripts/provision_tools.py:393,410–416`). A narrow shared-launcher safeguard is supported: SpacerPlacer and evOr select MAFFT from their isolated PATH and inherit subprocess environments (`tools/SpacerPlacer/sp_model/mafft_integration.py:340–347,368–383`; `tools/evOr/sp_model/mafft_integration.py:334–341,362–377`). This is shared runtime plumbing evidence only, not another scientific audit. Regenerate identities through the release process; do not patch an active runtime or combine their Python environments.

**P2 — preserve pair-type counts in the frontend model.** `normalizeRnaFamily()` passes a count object to array-only `labels()` (`frontend/src/features/results/model/rnaFamily.js:42–50`; `contextValues.js:12–16`). A fresh Node probe turned `{AU:2,GC:1}` into `[]`. The current component does not render this property and full downloads preserve it, so the impact is latent model corruption. Acceptance: retain a bounded validated pair-type/count mapping, reject invalid values without manufacturing zero, preserve missing values, and add a focused regression. No display expansion is required.

## Complete evidence, compact policy and actual connections

The public report preserves original/RNA hashes, representative crosswalk, aligned sequences, column counts, consensus/structure, energies, pair-type counts and retained probabilities (`backend/src/crispr_web/summaries/rna_family.py:9–25`). A preview may cap pairs and omit columns/probabilities, but full JSON, alignment FASTA, consensus DBN and pair TSV are built from full validated evidence (`publication/rna_family.py:11–55`). Raw `rna-family/work` files are intentionally excluded from download traversal (`publication/contracts.py:62`); their scientific content survives in allowlisted exports. “Complete evidence” must not promise raw work files in the ZIP.

The compact projector currently includes almost the complete public family report (`publication/compact/rna_family.py:4–6`). This accords with the module-specific promise of columns/probabilities in `docs/module-results.md:63`, but conflicts with generic wording placing software/execution metadata elsewhere (`:88–94`). The council rejected wholesale probability/column removal: this is a thresholded pair list, not the dense native matrix, and column counts describe observed support. **P2 documentation acceptance:** clarify the RNA-family exception and distinguish scientific counts from redundant metadata. Any later metadata-only projection change must preserve FFT-NS-2 interpretation, source membership, missingness and all promised evidence, updating its schema/examples/tests coherently. Also distinguish historical MAFFT 7.505 testing in the tool README (`:24–25`) from managed and freshly verified 7.526.

The input port is `kind: rna`, accepting matching declared DNA or RNA (`backend/src/crispr_web/exchange.py:23,77–93,133–151`). The continuation selector recognizes only typed arrays/repeats/spacers and `input-sequences.json`; it does not consume `result.json` or derive families from candidate reports (`frontend/src/features/results/useSequenceHandoff.js:16–32`). Replay republishes original normalized inputs (`publication/sequence_ports.py:42–63,90–105`). Origin annotations survive, but their schema lacks candidate identity, transcription orientation and boundary-evidence fields (`exchange.py:52–58`), and client annotations are not verified ancestry.

Consequently, a future curated candidate handoff needs explicit selection, orientation and boundary provenance plus independent admission checks. Shared subtype, duplicate repeat copies or alternative strand hypotheses cannot automatically establish a family. An alignment consensus containing gaps or `N` is not an observed transcript or an automatically valid next-module input. Existing UI wording correctly preserves these prerequisites (`frontend/src/components/submission/AnalysisForm.vue:116–141`).

## Deliberation, verification and limits

The science reviewer challenged treating fixed FFT-NS-2 or disabled RIBOSUM as bugs. The [MAFFT manual](https://mafft.cbrc.jp/alignment/software/manual/manual.html) and [Bernhart et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2621365/) support evaluating alternative strategies, not replacing this baseline without scope-specific evidence. Resolution: use independently curated reference structures, label experimental versus computational support, stratify diversity/redundancy, and compare held-out pair accuracy, calibration and resource costs before a versioned scientific change. Predictions made by the same folding pipeline are not independent truth. Both specialists accepted the helper-identity repair while rejecting claims of demonstrated biological error.
