# Scientific scope and release gates

Version 1 supplies descriptive thermodynamic evidence. It is not a calibrated
predictor of processing or biological activity. The biological question is
whether surrounding sequence changes support for an explicit repeat pair set.

Reimann et al. (NAR 2017, doi:10.1093/nar/gkw786) provide system-specific
experimental precedent. Reproduction requires their exact fragments, synthetic
additions and original settings; those differ from the general local-window
preset. A broader processing claim requires independent experimental studies
and improvements over isolated-fold and composition baselines. No published
accuracy value is transferred to this release.

Before enabling a predictive strand, subtype or family method, freeze its model,
training inventory, label vocabulary, reference provenance and benchmark splits.
Keep reverse complements, repeat homologues and all copies from one locus
together. Report selective error/coverage and unsupported-family abstention.
Unknown training overlap prevents an honest family-held-out claim.

No calibrated classifier, broad reference collection or independent biological
accuracy benchmark is included in version 1. Optional user-supplied references
provide inspectable retrieval; their quality remains tied to their provenance.
No nearest-reference hit is silently converted into a classification.
